Decoder-only Large View Synthesis Models (LVSMs) with KV-cache have recently achieved state-of-the-art quality by regressing novel views from neural networks without reconstructing 3D geometry, while Test-Time Training (TTT) layers save computation but sacrifice quality. We introduce a geometric construction whose terminal configuration determines the kernel and cokernel of a morphism between positive Lagrangian branes in the derived Fukaya category of a Calabi-Yau manifold. Thus, if the construction always terminates, the full subcategory of positive Lagrangian branes is abelian, as conjectured by Joyce. We show that this abelian category admits Harder-Narasimhan filtrations with respect to the central charge defined by the holomorphic volume form.
