Dynamics of a Single-Domain Dissipative Magnetic Gyroscope in Electric and Magnetic Fields arXiv Quantum & Condensed Matter (quant-ph, cond-mat)

We formulate the coupled spin--rotation dynamics of a single-domain symmetric top ferromagnetic needle (a magnetic gyroscope) in static magnetic and electric fields. The conservative Hamiltonian contains the rotational kinetic energy, the magnetocrystalline and shape anisotropy, the Zeeman energy, the electrostatic energy of the dipole induced in the conducting needle, the linear magnetoelectric coupling, and the relativistic motional (spin-orbit) coupling. Gilbert damping is introduced, and in an electric field, Debye relaxation of the induced electric dipole of the conducting needle also results in dissipation.
