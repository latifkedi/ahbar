Peak state transfer in trees and the cost of distance arXiv Mathematics (NT, CO, AG, LO, AT, GR)

There is peak state transfer between vertices u and v in a graph G if there exists a time t for which |(mathrme\mathrm i t M)u,v| saturates the triangle inequality imposed by its spectral decomposition and M is either the adjacency or Laplacian matrix. We show that peak state transfer at distance d requires fracd324(1 + o(1)) edges in the adjacency case, Ω(d2) edges in the Laplacian case, and that the leading coefficient in the former is tight. We find a family of graphs with Laplacian peak state transfer at distance d and Θ(d5) edges, showing that peak state transfer is achievable in a polynomial graph size.
