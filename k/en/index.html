Python's experimental just-in-time (JIT) compiler , which was added to CPython in 2024 for the 3.13 release , is on its way out. In June, the Python steering council announced that it was pausing work on the JIT in CPython's main branch until there was an accepted Python Enhancement Proposal (PEP) that addressed concerns about maintenance, compatibility, and set clear success metrics as well as a timeline for completion. After creating PEP 836 ("JIT Go Brrr: The Path to a Supported JIT Compiler for CPython") to try to meet the council's request, and after months of discussion, the JIT maintainers have withdrawn the PEP.

Optimal quantum discrimination at Liouvillian exceptional points. Central to non-Hermitian sensing is the question of whether exceptional points (EPs) can make weak perturbations easier to detect. arXiv Quantum & Condensed Matter (quant-ph, cond-mat)

Central to non-Hermitian sensing is the question of whether exceptional points (EPs) can make weak perturbations easier to detect. We show that despite enhanced spectral response, in finite-dimensional Lindblad systems with a unique stationary state and a nonzero relaxation gap, EPs offer no fundamental advantage in how the observation time required for reliable detection of a weak perturbation scales with the perturbation strength. We nevertheless demonstrate that by selectively choosing perturbations that enhance the quantum Fisher information rate, EPs can shorten the observation time needed to reach a predefined detection accuracy relative to a comparable non-EP reference at the same decay rate.
