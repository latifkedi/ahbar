P^2-MOPD: Accelerating Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation via Parameter Priors Hugging Face Daily Papers

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation consolidates expert capabilities by supervising student-generated trajectories, whereas task arithmetic offers a training-free alternative through linear parameter combinations. We view the linear combination of student and expert parameters as a single differentiable model and propose P^2-MOPD, which jointly optimizes the mixing coefficients and the student weights under the same on-policy distillation objective. Unlike task arithmetic, which stays within the span of the frozen student and expert parameters for any choice of merging coefficients, P^2-MOPD keeps the student trainable, so the experts act as parameter priors without confining it to this span.

Harper's beyond square-root conjecture for random matrices arXiv Mathematics (NT, CO, AG, LO, AT, GR)

For a Haar unitary matrix UN of size N, we prove that the first absolute moment of operatornametr(operatornameSymk UN) tends to zero as min(N,k)→∞. This settles the random matrix version of Harper's beyond square-root conjecture formulated by Wang and Xu, with no restriction on k/N. The proof is elementary and self-contained, combining exact weighted trace moments with a Gaussian change of measure.
