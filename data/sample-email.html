Making a conference poster is visual coding in miniature: an entire paper must be compressed onto a single page, which exercises summarization, visual design, and code implementation at once, using nothing beyond the paper itself. Spectral networks, finite webs, and the T_3 cubic differential · arXiv Mathematics (NT, CO, AG, LO, AT, GR)

We describe spectral networks for cubic differentials on \mathbf{P}^1 with exactly three poles all of order two, following observations of Hollands-Neitzke. To do so, we give a precise definition and establish some basic properties of a class of finite webs (corresponding to BPS states in physics) in the cubic differential setting, including their non-vanishing central charge. Turning to the example, we prove that at rational angles the network is totally degenerate; the number of walls and Stokes regions is explicitly computed and depends on an arithmetic criterion.
