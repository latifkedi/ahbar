Spectral networks, finite webs, and the T_3 cubic differential · arXiv Mathematics (NT, CO, AG, LO, AT, GR)

We describe spectral networks for cubic differentials on \mathbf{P}^1 with exactly three poles all of order two, following observations of Hollands-Neitzke. To do so, we give a precise definition and establish some basic properties of a class of finite webs (corresponding to BPS states in physics) in the cubic differential setting, including their non-vanishing central charge. Turning to the example, we prove that at rational angles the network is totally degenerate; the number of walls and Stokes regions is explicitly computed and depends on an arithmetic criterion.

Market Efficiency in a Changing Asset Population: Mobility, Entry, and Survival over Cryptocurrency's Full History · arXiv Economics (econ.EM, econ.GN, econ.TH)

Weak-form efficiency implies symmetry restrictions on the Markov chain governing movement across the cryptocurrency capitalisation distribution. We test them on the full listed population: 8,970 assets, 690 weekly cross-sections, 2013 to 2026, with entry, exit and survival observed rather than inferred. Symmetry is rejected in every era, but the asymmetry falls by seventy percent, and what converged is entry-driven compositional churn rather than coins' own valuations.
